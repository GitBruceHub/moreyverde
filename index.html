Rental Options

Flexible rental configurations are available depending on your stay requirements. The property can be rented as a full 3-bedroom residence, the upper-level main home, or the private studio apartment. Each option provides a different level of space, privacy, and access, allowing the property to accommodate everything from individual stays to extended group occupancy.

Pricing is not fixed due to the number of variables involved. Rates vary based on length of stay, time of year, occupancy level, and whether utilities such as RECO (electric), water, house keeping are included or billed separately. Additional considerations such as long-term discounts, seasonal demand, and multi-unit usage (main home + studio) also impact final pricing.

Because of these variables, the most accurate way to obtain current pricing and availability is to contact Property Management (PM) directly. This ensures you receive a tailored quote based on your specific dates, occupancy needs, and preferred rental configuration.

Points of interests that are accessible within minutes of your door.

Living at Vista Azul places you in the center of everything that makes Roatán one of the Caribbean’s most sought-after destinations. Within minutes, you can be relaxing on the white sands of West Bay Beach, Mesoamerican Barrier Reef, or diving world-famous sites like the Blue Channel, Mary’s Place, Texas & Pablos, West End Wall, and the island’s 150+ marked dive locations. Adventure seekers will appreciate proximity to South Shore Zipline, where canopy rides offer panoramic jungle and ocean views, as well as the popular eco-experiences at Gumbalimba Park and Daniel Johnson’s Sloth Hangout.

For those who enjoy exploring beyond the beach, scenic drives lead to Camp Bay Beach on the island’s east end, while cultural excursions through the Oakridge Mangrove Tunnels provide a glimpse into Roatán’s historic fishing communities. Evening outings are equally convenient, whether enjoying sunset cocktails at the Roatán Island Brewery or waterfront dining throughout West End village. The surrounding area blends marine biodiversity, adventure tourism, and relaxed island living into one cohesive lifestyle.

Owning property in this location means more than acquiring a home; it means positioning yourself within Roatán’s thriving tourism corridor. Vacation renters consistently search for accommodations near ziplining, reef diving, sloth encounters, and West Bay’s beaches.
